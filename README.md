# 🌾 CropX AI – Smart Agriculture Advisory Agent

CropX AI is an AI-powered smart agriculture platform designed to help farmers make better farming decisions using real-time data, AI guidance, soil testing, pest detection, and market insights. 

---

# 🚀 Problem Statement

Farmers face multiple challenges such as:

* Unpredictable weather
* Pest attacks
* Wrong fertilizer usage
* Lack of soil testing access
* Market price fluctuations
* Language barriers

These problems often reduce crop quality and overall production. 

---

# 💡 Solution

CropX AI provides a complete smart farming ecosystem where farmers can:

* Detect crop pests using AI
* Get crop recommendations
* Request soil testing from labs
* View real-time market insights
* Check weather forecasts
* Chat with AI assistant
* Download soil reports
* Calculate fertilizer requirements

The platform is fully mobile responsive and supports both Hindi and English languages.

# 🔄 Complete Workflow

## 👨‍🌾 Farmer Workflow

### Step 1 – Authentication

Farmer creates account or logs into the application.

### Step 2 – Dashboard Access

After login, farmer dashboard displays:

* Weather updates
* Market insights
* AI recommendations
* Soil testing status
* Notifications
* Pest detection tools

### Step 3 – Pest Detection

Farmer uploads crop image and AI detects:

* Diseases
* Pest attacks
* Crop issues

AI also provides prevention suggestions.

### Step 4 – Crop Guidance

Crop suggestions are generated based on:

* Soil type
* Weather conditions
* Farming season

### Step 5 – AI Chat Assistant

Farmers can directly chat with AI for:

* Farming advice
* Fertilizer guidance
* Disease prevention
* Crop planning

### Step 6 – Market Insights

Farmers can view:

* Current market prices
* 7-day crop trends
* Demand analysis
* Growth predictions
* Different crop comparisons

### Step 7 – Weather Forecast

Real-time weather information including:

* Temperature
* Rain forecast
* Humidity
* Wind conditions

### Step 8 – Soil Testing Request

Farmer submits soil testing request directly from the app.

### Step 9 – Lab Approval

Nearby lab admin receives request notification and approves it.

### Step 10 – Soil Sample Collection

Lab collects soil sample from farmer’s location.

### Step 11 – Soil Testing

Sample is tested in laboratory.

### Step 12 – Report Upload

Lab admin uploads final soil report to the platform.

### Step 13 – Report Download

Farmer downloads soil report directly from dashboard.

### Step 14 – AI Suggestions Based on Report

AI analyzes soil report and provides:

* Crop recommendations
* Fertilizer suggestions
* Soil improvement advice

### Step 15 – Fertilizer Calculator

Farmers can calculate:

* NPK requirements
* Seasonal fertilizer needs
